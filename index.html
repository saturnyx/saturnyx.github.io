What I love

  • Building ground up: I don't like just reusing old algorithms and libraries. I build my own customized implementations.
  • Performance is key: Pretty obvious for a Iot dev, but I like to make sure my code is optimized and efficient. I'll write a program in assembly if its a second faster.
  • Stability and Robustness: As someone who has knows what its like for important systems to fail, I make sure that all my libraries are stable and robust regardless of how small they are.
